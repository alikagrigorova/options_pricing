# Stage A summary: American options with daily exercise, puts and calls

Full tables: `report.md` (generated by `experiments/american_daily_report.py`);
data: `raw/`. Not part of the paper.

## Runs
- Check sets: 35 puts (the production check's) + 35 calls, 100 labels each, two
  variants with paired seeds: **D2-daily** (value-function label at t1) and
  **D2-daily-vf50** (`MultiConfig(vf_time=0.02)`: value-function label at the
  date nearest 1/50 year, the rule applied before it).
- 1,000 random puts + 1,000 random calls (Sobol points 20,000-21,999), one label
  each, both variants.
- References: continuous American PDE (target) and daily-Bermudan PDE.

## Findings
1. **vf50 cuts the noise** of a daily label by about 4x for Gamma and Theta and
   2x for Delta, Vanna, dDelta/dr, dDelta/dd (check sets, median noise of one
   label: put Gamma 35 % -> 9 %, Theta 66 % -> 15 %, Delta 2.2 % -> 1.0 %), with
   no bias cost. At 50 dates a year it is the current label exactly.
2. **Discreteness gap is small.** American - daily Bermudan (PDE): put price
   median +0.03 % (max 0.14 %), calls +0.001 % (max 0.04 %); Rho/Phi up to
   0.5 %. The put price shows -0.03 % (t -3.3) against American and -0.01 %
   (not significant) against daily Bermudan over 1,000 points: the remaining
   put price bias is mostly this gap.
3. **Puts (vf50, 1,000 points vs American):** price -0.026 +- 0.008 %, Delta
   +0.10 +- 0.04 %, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho, Phi, Volga, Vanna not significant.
   The hybrid works as at 50 dates a year: oos price -0.23 %, ins +0.18 %.
4. **Calls have a method bias in the price**: -0.07 % over 1,000 points (t -8.6,
   the same against both references), up to -0.4 % at T = 2, sigma = 40 %
   (check options 59-61), where the out-of-sample label recovers only about
   half the early-exercise premium. Diagnostic
   (`diagnostics/call_via_put_symmetry.*`, 12 labels per option): the same
   options labelled as puts by put-call symmetry, C(S, K, r, d) = P(K, S, d, r),
   with the Greeks mapped by homogeneity, are unbiased (price -0.02 %, -0.00 %;
   Delta -0.08 %, -0.04 %).
5. **A smaller bias where early exercise is allowed but never pays** (low sigma,
   in the money calls with d < r, premium 0): -0.14 % on the price, both oos and
   ins low; the symmetric put label has it too. Within the 0.2 % criterion.
6. **Volga, Vanna:** the in-sample Volga is biased up (+90 % to +235 %), so the
   hybrid's choice of oos for Volga holds at daily dates.
7. **Time and memory (SCC):** about 45 / 72 / 160 s per label at T = 0.5 / 1 / 2
   (mean 76-83 s over the random points), peak 0.7 GB; PDE references 29 s mean
   (both styles), 65 s max.

## Proposals (not implemented)
- Use **vf50** for Stage B.
- Make **call labels from put labels by put-call symmetry** (exact for American
  and Bermudan options): one put label at spot K, strike S0, rate d, yield r;
  Delta_C = (P - K Delta_P) / S0, Gamma_C = K^2 Gamma_P / S0^2, Vega and Volga
  equal, Vanna_C = (Vega_P - K Vanna_P) / S0, Rho_C = Phi_P, Phi_C = Rho_P,
  Theta equal, dDelta/dr and dDelta/dd mapped likewise. Same cost as a direct
  call label.
- Stage B cost (vf50): about 440 CPU hours of labels + 160 of references for
  20,000 points; 1 core and 2-4 GB per task, 3 h limit for 400 tasks.
- For calls, widen d to [0, 8 %] with extra points in d in (0, 3 %) (the edge
  of the no-exercise region for calls is d = 0).
