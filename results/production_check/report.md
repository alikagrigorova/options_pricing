Not part of the paper. `experiments/tournament.py --part production_check`, `experiments/production_check_report.py`.

**Settings.** `MultiConfig()`: design D2 (runs (S, σ), (S, r), (S, d); price, Δ, Γ averaged over the runs), exercise rule fitted out of sample on the pilot paths, 100,000 main + 100,000 pilot paths per run, α_S and α_σ by the paper's selector (M = ν + 1) on the pilot, α_r = α_d = min(0.03, r0) (0.03 for every option here; the d0 rule min(0.03, d0) gives the same windows for these options), exact European labels at r0 ≤ 0, d0 ≥ 0. 100 replications per option; seeds `job_seed(8004, {id}, rep)`, a fresh SeedSequence per label, so every label is reproducible.
