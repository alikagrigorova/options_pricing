# Stage 2 pilot: default label (D2, hybrid) against the PDE reference

Not part of the paper. `experiments/tournament.py --part stage2 / stage2_ref / stage2_noise`, `experiments/stage2_report.py`.

Inputs: scrambled Sobol sequence (5 dimensions, seed 20261011; `experiments/tournament.py`, `inputs`), the
tournament's part B ranges; the first 2,500 points are the tournament options.

| input | range |
|---|---|
| S0 | 40 (labels scale exactly with S0 and K) |
| moneyness S0/K | uniform on [0.85, 1.20]; K = S0 / m rounded to 0.01 |
| σ | uniform on [0.10, 0.40], rounded to 0.001 |
| T | uniform on [0.25, 2.00] years, rounded to 0.001 |
| d | uniform on [0, 0.04], rounded to 0.0001 |
| r | 2 % of the points at r = 0; 25 % uniform on [0.0025, 0.0275]; 73 % uniform on [0.03, 0.06]; rounded to 0.0025 |

**Label.** `MultiConfig()`: design D2, 100,000 main + 100,000 pilot paths per run, α_S and α_σ by the selector, α_r = min(0.03, r0), α_d = min(0.03, d0), exact European labels at r0 ≤ 0 and d0 ≥ 0. Default label (plain columns): the average of the out-of-sample and in-sample labels for price, Δ, Γ, Θ, Vega, Rho, Phi; the out-of-sample label for Volga, Vanna, delta_r, delta_d. The oos, ins and avg labels are stored as `<greek>_oos`, `_ins`, `_avg`; `default_source` names the source of each plain column. One label per point, seeds `job_seed(8006, {id}, 0)`; noise check seeds `job_seed(8007, {id}, rep)`. Reference: `reference.label_reference` (Crank–Nicolson).

Points: 500 labelled, 500 references; 16 left out of the statistics because the label is exact by construction (11 at r0 = 0; 5 exercised at t0 in label and reference). Exercise-region disagreements (label ≠ reference): 1.

Check at r0 = 0 (exact European labels against the PDE): largest |MC − PDE| price 0, delta 5.6e-17, gamma 3.5e-18, theta 4.4e-16, vega 1.8e-15, volga 8.9e-16, vanna 5.6e-17, rho 0, phi 3.6e-15, delta_r 2.2e-16, delta_d 0.

Bias = mean of (MC − PDE) over the points; se = sd / √n over the points (it holds the label noise and the spread of the bias between points); t = bias / se. **Bold**: |t| > 3.
